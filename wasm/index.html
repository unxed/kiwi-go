kiwi-go WebAssembly Demo

This demo runs the Cassowary solver entirely in your browser using Go + WebAssembly.

Try it: Drag the bottom-right corner of the gray container below to resize it.

  • Box 1 is attached to the left.
  • Box 2 is attached directly to the right of Box 1.
  • The width of Box 2 is constrained to always be exactly twice the width of Box 1.
  • The sum of their widths always equals the container's width.
